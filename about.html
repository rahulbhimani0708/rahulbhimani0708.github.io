My Journey

Hi there! I'm Rahul Bhimani, an AI Researcher, Instructor, and Developer passionate about building the next generation of intelligent systems that merge learning, reasoning, and autonomy.

As the Founder of SkillSync.ai, my mission is to bridge the gap between human intelligence and artificial intelligence by designing advanced AI Agents, Fine-Tuned LLMs, and Multimodal Learning Systems that empower education, businesses, and developers to evolve together with AI.

I believe that AI mastery should be universal — creating a world where anyone can understand, build, and enhance AI using accessible, open, and scalable tools. My long-term goal is to make AI literacy and mastery accessible to every learner, from students to professionals.
